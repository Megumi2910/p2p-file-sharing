Manual acceptance

  1. Record OS, Java, Maven, VM network mode, addresses, ports, and configs.
  2. Build with mvn clean verify (or mvn clean package for packaging only) and launch only produced JARs.
  3. Prove discovery in both directions and retain tracker logs.
  4. Transfer empty, sub-chunk, exact-chunk, multi-chunk, Unicode-named, and duplicate-named files.
  5. Reject an offer and stop one peer during a transfer.
  6. Compare source and destination SHA-256 values.
  7. Restart and confirm stale presence disappears and ports rebind.
