2026-09-06 — Sequential transfer state machine and progress accounting (F-05)

Transfer / State Machine

  • Why: Enforce strict sequential stop-and-wait transfer invariants, prevent duplicate or out-of-order chunk processing, and provide truthful monotonically confirmed progress accounting (R-004, R-005).
  • Behavior: Sender validates source is a readable regular file, checks whole-file SHA-256 before completion, enforces 3-retry limit on hash rejection, and requires transfer ID matching on all responses. Receiver validates transfer ID, chunk layout (offset and length), per-chunk SHA-256, and handles repeat of immediately accepted chunk by re-acknowledging without rewriting or incrementing progress. Conflicting repeats or early completion frames are rejected. Progress percent is overflow-safe, showing 100% for completed empty files and 0% for non-completed empty state.
